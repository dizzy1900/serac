### Design choices (not observations)

Source-zone bounding boxes used for the crops, chosen for this project (W, S, E, N):

- `lhende-khola-trishuli`: 85.51, 28.27, 85.53, 28.29 (cube EPSG:32645); DEM crop buffer 2000 m
- `chamoli-rishiganga`: 79.68, 30.33, 79.8, 30.42 (cube EPSG:32644); DEM crop buffer 0 m
- `blatten-lotschental`: 7.78, 46.39, 7.87, 46.45 (cube EPSG:32632); DEM crop buffer 0 m

Sentinel-2 fixture window: 376680..379240 E, 3359180..3361740 N in EPSG:32644 (2 560 m square on the 20 m grid, 256x256 px at 10 m / 128x128 px at 20 m), centred near Ronti Peak; smaller than the AOI bbox so each band stays under ~150 KB. DEM crops are
the AOI bbox (plus buffer) snapped outward to the GLO-30 1 arc-second grid, EPSG:4326
float32 as delivered. S2 scene selection: reprocessings collapsed to the newest
baseline, ranked by the fraction of SCL classes {3, 8, 9, 10, 11} over the fixture
window; two pre-event and one post-event scene around 2021-02-07
(see `sentinel2/chamoli-rishiganga/candidates.json`).
